# Requirements: TrendCast — v0.1.7 Dashboard UX Polish (ARCHIVED)

**Defined:** 2026-08-30
**Shipped:** 2026-08-30
**Core Value:** Surface the strongest, most reliable signal of what prediction markets are moving and why — by correlating social hype, news, and market odds — fast enough that the user trusts it as a daily decision aid.

> Note: v0.1.7 was executed as a UX-polish milestone driven by a live screenshot review; requirements were captured as UX-01..UX-07 in the roadmap rather than a formal REQUIREMENTS.md at milestone start. This archive records them with final outcomes.

## v0.1.7 Requirements

### Feed Card & Header Polish (Phase 17)

- [x] **UX-01**: News tab cards use a neutral surface with a per-source accent (left border + source label color) instead of full saturated backgrounds; no two adjacent sources look identical; text contrast ≥ WCAG AA on both themes — **Validated** (NewsFeed rework, visually verified)
- [x] **UX-02**: News cards show article publish time (from `publishedAt`), not the collection batch time; keyword chips capped at 3 with a `+N` overflow indicator — **Validated**
- [x] **UX-03**: Hype Feed virality score visually prominent (heat-colored badge + thin score bar); X/TikTok cards show engagement metrics when available — **Validated** (HypeFeed rework)
- [x] **UX-04**: Build version string out of the dashboard header (visible in Settings); last collection renders as relative time with absolute time on hover — **Validated** ("Last collected Xm ago", title attr)

### Navigation & Density (Phase 18)

- [x] **UX-05**: Tab navigation groups tabs under small section labels (Data / Insights / More) while keeping single-click access — **Validated**; scope extended: tabs consolidated 11 → 7 (watchlist → Markets, market-news → News segmented view, run history → Correlations, community+FAQ → Help)
- [x] **UX-06**: News cards show a sentiment indicator (colored dot: green/red/neutral) derived from the item's sentiment when available — **Validated**
- [x] **UX-07**: Density toggle (comfortable/compact) on Hype Feed and News tabs switches grid card size, persists in settings (`feedDensity`), defaults to comfortable — **Validated** (deep-merge backfill for existing users)

### Bug Fixes (discovered during milestone)

- [x] **UX-08**: Star click in Markets tab adds the market to the watchlist instead of navigating to the market URL — **Validated** (`preventDefault` + `stopPropagation` on star wrapper in both views)
- [x] **UX-09**: e2e suite matches the real tab layout — **Validated** (rewritten for 7 tabs; removed stale 9-tab assertions and version-in-header assertion)
