<objective>
Make ticker forms bridge across the correlation pipeline: `extractKeywords` emits bare cashtag forms, `keywordSimilarity` strips `$` so legacy stored data bridges without migration, `extractEntities` recognizes bare all-caps tickers under strict gates and unifies cashtag/bare/org-name entities onto one canonical key, and the engine's cashtag boost detection stops keying on the now-dead `startsWith('$')` string shape.

Purpose: CORR-01 + CORR-02 — a social signal saying `$AMZN` must correlate with stock-indicator news saying `AMZN — Stock Indicator` at both entity and keyword level, where today the two spaces are disjoint (`$amzn` ∩ `amzn` = ∅; entity keys `amzn` vs `amazon` never intersect).

Output: Canonicalized extraction tier (`keywords.ts`, `entities.ts`), reworked boost detection (`correlation.ts`), display-parity topic labels (`alerts.ts`), lockstep-updated oracles/fixtures, and a new `tests/unit/bridging.test.ts` proving the bridged match end-to-end.
</objective>
