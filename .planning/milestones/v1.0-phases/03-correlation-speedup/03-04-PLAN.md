<objective>
Add equivalence tests for the embedding and NER engines. These two engines use **semantic** similarity (embedding cosine similarity) and **ML-extracted entity** similarity respectively — both can match a contract with zero keyword overlap, so a keyword-only inverted index is NOT a valid superset of their matches. Applying the index would break the locked D-02 equivalence requirement. This plan therefore keeps them on the naive loop and adds equivalence tests that (a) prove their output is correct against a hand-verified oracle and (b) document why the index does not apply.

Purpose: D-01 mandates equivalence coverage for ALL engines. Embedding and NER are the two engines where the index is provably inapplicable — the equivalence test is the guard that proves their output is stable and correct, and prevents a future contributor from incorrectly routing them through the keyword index.
Output: `tests/unit/embedding-equivalence.test.ts`, `tests/unit/ner-equivalence.test.ts`.

> **SC1/SC3 partial-trace note:** Success criteria 1 (candidate-filtered index) and 3 (both heuristic and ML paths use the same index) are satisfied **for the keyword-overlap engines only** (heuristic, zeroshot, sentiment, llm). Embedding and NER remain on the naive loop by necessity — a keyword-only index is not a valid superset for semantic/entity matching, and routing them through it would break locked D-02. Their dominant cost is already batched embedding/extraction, not the O(n²) comparison, so the latency goal is still met. The verifier should not fail SC1/SC3 for these two engines.
</objective>
